# Panel icons and hover states

Native world panels use a selected subset of [Pixelarticons](https://github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons), pinned to revision `8275e0af7c16aa40c54ea2b90b7af83b1fe4eb4c`. The source icons are MIT licensed and their notice is packaged as `META-INF/PIXELARTICONS-LICENSE.txt`.

The floating pill's title always begins at its left edge. Its drag/background region fills the header up to the close button, including the extra width exposed during expansion. The close button retains its stable world position. The whole pill uses PanelSurface filtering, so Panel smoothing toggles both text/icon and background sampling together.

The runtime asset is `assets/winlandcraft/textures/gui/pixelarticons.png`: an 8-column by 3-row atlas of white 24×24 icons with binary alpha. `PixelIcon` order is the atlas order. `PanelCanvas` applies color at the vertices, so normal, hover, active, and disabled states reuse the same texture without additional texture binds. Keep the atlas dimensions, enum order, packaged notice, and `verifyMixinPackaging` checks synchronized when changing it.

Local pointer coordinates are recorded once by `WorldPanel.pointerMoved` before the panel-specific hover callback. Native controls use `hovered` or `hoverColor`; browser content still receives its normal CEF mouse move. This keeps visual hit regions aligned with input regions and clears the state when the pointer leaves or the panel closes.

The resize cursor reuses the same atlas. Cyan diagonal arrows plus the expand icon identify viewport-resolution resize; yellow arrows plus the scale icon identify physical scaling.

Floating titlebars scale with camera distance: one logical pixel occupies at least 0.001 world units per block of distance, retaining the original near-view minimum. Text, buttons, spacing, and hit geometry scale together without resizing the app. Distance is bounded by the existing 4096-block move limit. The edge activation band also grows with distance but is capped relative to panel size. Scale is frozen during movement/curve grabs and updated when released, preserving the grab point and slider mapping.

App window chrome is a local `EdgeControls` panel outside the nearest content edge. Activation requires a continuous 500ms dwell on the same app edge; leaving or changing sides resets it. The compact header is 240x40; hover expands to 360x112 with Curve and optional Ungroup. Expansion grows outward while preserving the header/close world positions. Rendering and picking share header offsets. Along-edge following uses exponential smoothing with a 120ms time constant and pauses while directly hovered, moved, or curving. Opacity fades over 200ms, with a 650ms leave grace period; reversing a fade preserves its current opacity. Nearly invisible controls cannot intercept clicks. Curved owners use the exact GroupCurve surface point and tangent orientation. No new atlas entries are needed. Shell panels and remote replicas do not expose placement controls.
